# How to use the app

For the **project manager**, the **quantity surveyor** and the
**administrator**. Plain language, nothing you do not need.

Sign in at **vo.osmanflow.com**. Your menu: **Overview · My Tasks · Variations ·
Blocked**, plus **Reports** for commercial people and **Projects, Company,
Users, Permissions** for admins.

**Start every day on Overview.** The top of it is a list called *Needs Action
Today*: one row per thing, each saying what to do, who owes it, how much money
is behind it and when it is due. Red rows first. That list is the whole job.

---

## The Overview, and the seven words on every change

The top of **Overview** is **Needs Action Today**: one row per thing that needs
doing, each saying what to do, who owes it, the money and the date. Red first.
Filter it to **Mine** and that is your day.

Above it are at most four figures. Below it, the workflow and a line per
project. Anything to do with retention, invoicing or payment is on **Reports**.


```
New change → PM review → QS pricing → PM approval → Sent to client → Client decision → Closed
```

The **initial notice** runs beside this, on its own track. It never holds up
pricing.

## Project Manager

### 1. Decide the notice

A change appears on **Overview** as a row saying **Decide if a notice is
needed**. Click it. The **Review change** card holds everything in one place: what changed, the engineer's own words, the date,
whether work has started, the evidence, and the **days remaining** on the
notice.

Three answers:

| | What it needs | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Yes — send initial notice** | Nothing | A **draft** notice appears. Nothing goes to the client yet |
| **No — notice not required** | A reason, from the list | No notice. Reason saved on the record |
| **Need more information** | Say what is missing | The engineer gets a task quoting your words |

**The QS can start pricing straight away** on Yes and on No. On "Need more
information", only if you tick the box.

> The days remaining come from **this project's** notice period, not a fixed 28.

### 2. Send the notice

Open the notice. It opens as something to **read**, not to fill in.

1. Check the recipient, the reference and the deadline
2. **Edit notice** if the wording needs it → **Save draft**
3. **Send initial notice**

**Only that last button sends.** Choosing "Yes" in step 1 sent nothing.

Then watch the state: **Pending delivery → Delivered → Acknowledgement pending →
Acknowledged**. If it says **Delivery failed**, press **Retry delivery**.
Pricing carries on either way.

> "Delivered" means the mail system confirmed it. The app never says delivered
> just because it sent something.

### 3. Approve, which sends it

When the QS submits a price, open the change. One card holds the whole
decision: the scope, the notice and where it got to, the value, the time and the
evidence.

- **Approve and send final VO to client** — one press raises the VO, makes the
  PDF, files it, emails the client and starts their response clock.
- **Return it to the QS** — needs a reason, goes back to pricing.

If a required notice is not confirmed delivered you get a **red warning** naming
where it actually is. You can still approve. It warns; it does not block. That
you approved anyway is recorded.

## Quantity Surveyor

### 1. Price it

**Overview** → a row saying **Submit pricing** → the **pricing panel** on the
change. Filter the list to **QS pricing** to see only yours.

Add a line at a time: description, quantity, unit, rate, and **where the rate
came from** (contract BOQ, pro rata, star rate, quotation, daywork). Then the
preliminaries % and the overhead & profit %.

**Never type a total.** The app adds it up. Change one line and the total moves.

The panel tells you how many lines rest on a **star rate**. That is the number a
consultant attacks first.

### 2. Or say it is not a variation

**"Not a variation"** is a proper answer, not a failure. Use it when the work
was already in scope. It closes the change cleanly.

### 3. Submit

**Submit pricing** sends it to the PM. The value is then **frozen** — repricing
afterwards does not change what he is approving.

### 4. After the client agrees

The **money panel** on the change: raise the application, then the invoice,
record payments, raise a credit note if value comes back. Retention is held
automatically at the project's own percentage.

## Administrator

### Setting a company up, in this order

1. **Company** — the company's own details
2. **Users** — invite people, give each a role, set a password or email a link
3. **Projects** — create the project
4. **Contract rules** — notice period, claim period, client response window,
   follow-up on or off, retention. **These are the numbers the app actually
   uses. Get them right.**
5. **Team** — add the PM, the QS and the engineer
6. **Contacts** — add the client, tick what they are allowed to do, and set the
   **notice recipient** email
7. **Permissions** — only if the defaults do not fit the company

### Day to day

- **Somebody leaves → Deactivate, not Delete.** Deactivating keeps their name on
  their work. Delete is refused for anyone the records still point at.
- **The WhatsApp number belongs to one person.** Giving it to someone else
  **takes** it from the previous holder, on purpose.
- **Removing someone from a project is reversible** and keeps the history.
- **You can set the app up. You cannot send a notice.** A notice is a
  contractual act in the company's name, and the person who decided it is the
  person who sends it.

---

## Rules for everybody

- **Nothing is sent by accident.** Every outbound act is one button a person
  pressed.
- **Never type a total.** The app calculates every figure.
- **Do not invent.** "I do not know" is a fine answer. A guess on a date, a
  price or a clause is not.
- **What the engineer wrote is kept word for word.** Nothing is tidied away.
- **Photos and files are never replaced.** The original always stays.

## When something looks wrong

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| A project is missing | You are not on its team. An admin adds you |
| No button where this guide says | You do not have that permission |
| **Delivery failed** on a notice | Press **Retry delivery**. Pricing is unaffected |
| Something feels stuck | **Blocked** lists it, who owns the next action, how long it has waited, the money, and what to do |
| A WhatsApp photo did not arrive | Known gap. Use the web form or email for photos |
| An Arabic PDF | Not yet. Documents print in English |
